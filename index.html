Remix is a mobile app. It remakes the food you love in a lighter way, and it is honest about the trade-off. This policy describes the information the app handles. It is not medical, nutritional or legal advice. Allergen information in a recipe is a guide, not a guarantee. Always check labels.

1.Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Remix mobile application and to this website. This website only publishes the policy. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not use advertising or analytics scripts.

Remix is operated as the Remix app. This page does not state a registered company name, company number, or postal address, because those details are not published in the app.

2.Information you provide

When you use Remix, you can give us:

  • Where you heard about Remix, and the time you answered.
  • Your goal, what you crave, an eating style, dietary needs, allergies, the equipment in your kitchen, and your cooking confidence.
  • Whether you prefer metric or imperial units.
  • A dish name, a description, a photo, or a link to a post about a dish.
  • A star rating, tags, and notes on a recipe you cooked.
  • A share card you choose to create from a recipe.

3.Sign-in

You sign in with Apple or Google. Those services give Remix the account details needed to create your sign-in, which can include an email address and an account identifier. Remix stores your account with Supabase. We do not ask you to invent a separate Remix password in the app.

4.Photos and your library

If you allow it, Remix can use the camera to snap a dish, and your photo library to import a screenshot or packaging. A photo you submit is stored for your account so the app can show it again. The microphone permission exists only if the camera preview on your phone requires it. Remix does not use the microphone to record conversations.

If you create a share card, Remix can save that image to your photo library when you ask it to.

5.Links and videos

You can paste a link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or another page. Remix does not download those videos. When a video is shown, it is played with that service’s own player. The link, and a cover image if one can be fetched, may be stored with the remix job for your account.

6.Recipes, ratings and your kitchen

Remix saves the recipes it makes for you, the option you pick, and the ratings or notes you add. Your kitchen equipment and cooking confidence are used so the steps match what you have and how confident you are.

7.Allergies and dietary needs

Allergies and dietary needs are saved on your account and sent with a remix so the recipe can leave those items out. Allergies are treated as a hard exclusion in the recipe check. This is still a guide. Ingredients, labels, and kitchens change, and you should check them yourself.

Remix does not claim to be a medical device, and it does not provide personal medical advice.

8.How recipes are generated

To write a remix, the app sends the dish, any photo or link context you provided, and your relevant preferences (including allergies, diet, kitchen, skill, and goal) to a server function. That function asks Anthropic’s Claude model to draft the recipe. Nutrition totals for the remix are calculated from ingredient quantities. Original nutrition figures are estimates.

9.Notifications

If you allow notifications, Remix stores a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on your account so it can send a push notification to that device. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.

10.Analytics

Analytics runs only when a PostHog key is configured for the app. Remix then records product events such as starting or finishing onboarding, requesting a remix, saving a recipe, cooking, rating, adjusting a remix, or creating a share card. A referral answer can be attached to the finished-onboarding event. A dish name can be attached to a remix event. The signed-in account identifier is sent to PostHog so events from the same account can be grouped. PostHog may also record app open and background events. Analytics is not used on this website.

11.Community dishes

Remix can show trending dishes. Another person’s photo, account, personal notes, and private recipe are not shown on your trending list as their personal content. A trending card uses a generic dish and, where a starter image is used, an app image rather than a private photo from someone else’s library.

12.How we use information

Remix uses the information above to:

  • Create and keep your account signed in.
  • Remember the preferences you set during onboarding.
  • Generate, save, and show recipes for you.
  • Keep listed allergies out of those recipes.
  • Send a notification you have allowed.
  • Understand whether the app is working, when analytics is on.
  • Delete your account when you ask.

Remix does not sell your personal information. This website does not show advertisements.

13.Who else handles information

These services process information so the app can run. Each service’s own privacy policy describes what it does with information it receives.

  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Google, if you use Google sign-in, and Firebase for push notifications.
  • Supabase, for the account, database, file storage, and server functions.
  • Anthropic, to draft a recipe from the remix request.
  • PostHog, when product analytics is switched on.
  • TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube, only to play a video in that service’s player when you open one.

Those providers may process information in countries other than yours. The app does not publish a fixed data-centre region on this page.

14.How long we keep information

Account information and recipes are kept while your account exists. Photos stored for your account are removed when the account is deleted. Copies can remain in backups for a short time after that. A fixed backup period is not stated here because one is not published in the app.

15.Deleting your account

In the app you can delete your account. That asks the server to remove the photos stored for you and to delete the sign-in. Signing out clears private images kept on that device. You can also email support@remixai.com if deletion in the app does not work.

16.Your choices

You can:

  • Edit preferences, including allergies and diet, in the app.
  • Refuse the camera, photo library, or notifications. Related features then will not work.
  • Sign out.
  • Delete the account.
  • Email support@remixai.com to ask a question about your information or to ask for a copy or a correction. What we can do depends on the law that applies to you. This page does not name a country of establishment.

17.Children

Remix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email support@remixai.com.

18.Security

Account data is stored behind sign-in. Private recipe photos are loaded with time-limited links rather than a public folder. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

19.Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. The current version is the one published at this address.

20.Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: support@remixai.com

Remix does not publish a postal address on this page.
